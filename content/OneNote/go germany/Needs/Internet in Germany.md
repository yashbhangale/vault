**3. Where Can I Get Free Wi-Fi?**

- University campus: Usually available through university Wi-Fi or eduroam.
- Student dormitory (Studentenwohnheim): Internet may be included in rent.
- Shared apartment (WG): Broadband costs can be shared with flatmates.
- Public libraries: Often provide free Wi-Fi.
- Cafés and restaurants: Wi-Fi availability varies.

Important: Do not assume student accommodation includes internet. Confirm this before booking.

**5. My Recommended Setup**  
For a master's degree in Germany, especially with laptop-based DevOps, Kubernetes, cloud labs, and software downloads:

1. Confirm whether the accommodation includes broadband.
2. Buy a prepaid SIM after arriving in Germany.
3. Start with 10–25 GB mobile data if reliable Wi-Fi is available.
4. Choose 50 GB or more if you frequently use a mobile hotspot.
5. For large downloads and technical labs, use home broadband rather than relying entirely on mobile data.
6. Check network coverage at the university and accommodation before choosing a provider.
7. Check contract duration, activation charges, router fees, and the price after promotional periods.
 
**6. Important German Terms**

- SIM-Karte: SIM card
- WLAN: Wi-Fi
- Internetanschluss: Internet connection
- Studentenwohnheim: Student dormitory
- WG (Wohngemeinschaft): Shared apartment
- Flatrate: Fixed-price plan
- Prepaid: Pay in advance, generally without a long-term contract
- Mindestvertragslaufzeit: Minimum contract duration
