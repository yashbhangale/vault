**5. Loan Amount Assessment**  
**₹5 lakh — Lowest debt**

- Suitable if savings and other funding cover most expenses.
- May fall below SBI Global Ed-Vantage’s minimum loan amount; check eligibility for another scheme.

**₹10 lakh — Moderate borrowing**

- Potential compromise between personal savings and debt.
- Estimated EMI: ₹12,822–₹15,913.
- Requires a credible plan for the remaining costs.

**₹15 lakh — Significant commitment**

- Could be considered for a suitable, affordable public university.
- Estimated EMI: ₹19,233–₹23,870.
- Requires strong employment prospects and a repayment fallback plan.

**₹20 lakh — High commitment**

- Estimated EMI: ₹25,644–₹31,826.
- Higher exposure if employment is delayed or salary is lower than expected.

**₹25 lakh — Very high commitment**

- Estimated EMI: ₹32,054–₹39,783.
- Avoid unless the total cost is justified, funding is secure and repayment remains manageable under adverse scenarios.

**6. SBI Scheme Considerations**

- Global Ed-Vantage is intended for eligible full-time overseas programmes.
- The standard scheme has a stated minimum loan of ₹7.5 lakh.
- Collateral is generally required under the standard scheme; eligible select institutions may qualify for different terms.
- Interest rates are floating and can change.
- Processing fees and other charges apply; confirm the current schedule with SBI.
- A loan sanction does not automatically guarantee that the German student-visa financial requirements are met.

**7. Recommended Strategy**

1. Prioritise tuition-free public universities in Germany.
2. Build savings while continuing your current job.
3. Apply for admission before committing to a loan.
4. Calculate the genuine funding gap after accounting for savings, scholarships and eligible funding.
5. Borrow only what is necessary.
6. Do not rely on a German part-time job to cover mandatory funding or guarantee EMI repayment.
7. Keep an emergency reserve and a fallback repayment plan.
8. Verify the final loan terms and visa financial requirements before committing.

**Final Conclusion**  
**Initial target: explore a ₹5–10 lakh loan, but first calculate the actual funding gap.**  
Consider ₹15 lakh only if the programme’s cost and career prospects justify it. Borrowing ₹20–25 lakh creates substantially higher repayment risk and should require a stronger financial justification.  
All calculations are illustrative, based on 9.40% annual interest and the assumptions above. Obtain a personalised repayment schedule from SBI before making a decision.
