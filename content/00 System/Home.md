## A simple daily loop

1. **Capture:** use your usual Telegram or OneNote flow. Import changed OneNote pages into the vault before processing them.
2. **Act:** open your priorities or a project and choose the next action.
3. **Find:** search for the topic first; follow original sources when you need detail.
4. **Review:** open **Needs synthesis** in the processed browser and review useful captures. Keep low-value attachments for reference.
